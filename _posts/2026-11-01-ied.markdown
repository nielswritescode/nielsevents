## What, where and when?

I want to offer the oppurtunity to enjoy individual creative movement and being heard by others in a very accessible format. Make a regular day shine and fulfill some basic human needs 😊

This event might be better suited for people who have done experiential dance or sharing before - but beginners are welcome as well. Just maybe contact me before with any questions you might have. And never hesitate to contact me in the space (even if I seem busy, as I will be joining myself).

The event will be from 15-16 on the 29th of January 2026 in CREA room 402.
Address: Nieuwe Achtergracht 170, 1018 WV Amsterdam

## Schedule

15.00-15.30 Dance Experientially
_I'll very briefly guide us into the dance, setting the stage for a non-judgemental and aware attitude, and then it's up to you! I'd like to mention that this dance is individual. Feel free to observe, smile at or get inspired by others, but don't initiate contact. We'll dance to a nice short flow._
15.30-15.55 Sharing
_Share whatever is alive in you for 5 uninterrupted minutes with another person. Your partner will do an exercise in listening in presence without reacting. Non-verbal feedback is welcome to show, but don't show it on purpose. Then we switch_
15.55 Closure with the group

## Cost
This event is unconditionally given. That means it's a gift to the world from me, and I have no expectation of reciprocity (If I do somehow, please contact me with your feedback.)

That doesn't mean I don't welcome energy exchange. I'll lovingly accept your gift of food, money, massage or something else.

## Mentions
- I'm experimenting with formats and I would love your feedback. Knowing what worked for you and what didn't will help me in shaping these spaces. It is especially helpful for me to know how you felt during certain moments.
- I take responsibility for the space and for the exercises, but I expect from participants that they take responsibility for themselves and their own boundaries.
