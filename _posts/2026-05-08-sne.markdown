## Summary

To feel your cheek on a tree. To lie down, watch a cloud and hear the birds. To be imbued with the magic of the beautiful Gaaperplas. 

I keep track of time and facilitate an opening and a closing so you can feel nicely held. 

Truly diving into your somatic aspect can be seen as 'weird' by others and you will most likely be impacted by this. This is luckily something you can work on. I will provide some tips. The group also supports you in the social aspect of the somatic challenge!
