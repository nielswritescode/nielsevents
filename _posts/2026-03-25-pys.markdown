## What, where and when?

A semi-facilitated space for sharing, mindful time & pleasure yoga. A 2 hour bubble for spending quality time in presence. The event is more suited for intermediate practitioners, but beginners are always welcome. Bring your own mat!


The event will be between **11.15 and 13.30** on the **25th of March 2026** at **Common Ground Amsterdam**. 

Address: Zeeburgerdijk 265, 1095 AC Amsterdam We meet outside

## Structure

- 11.15-11.30 Walk in [*please be on time*]
- 11.30-12.00 1 on 1 uninterrupted sharing for 5 minutes with impact [guided]
- 12.00-12.30 Mindful Time [*unguided, silent*]
- 12.30-13.10 Pleasure Yoga [*unguided, with light ambient music*]
- 13.10-13.20 Closing Up


> Depending on the group I might include some intentional choosing dynamics. With whom do you feel drawn to share right now?

> About the **Sharing**: I will invite you to share freely for 5 minutes with a partner who listens in presence. The listener is not to keep a straight face nor show social feedback - they are simply to observe what is alive in them while they receive your sharing. You are to share freely - in any way you feel like. There is no obligation to make sense or to talk, even.

> **Mindful time** is meant for activities such as: mindful breakfast - music listening - meditation - tea drinking. What is something mindful that is usually in your morning routine? Most activities are welcome - but if you want to do something more exotic please contact me beforehand :) 

> I recently discovered a label for a practice I have been doing for 2 years: **Pleasure Yoga**. It is the art of moving your body in a way that feels good. Pleasureable. Whether it's lying in total stillness, massaging your face or rolling around on the floor. It is a shift in focus from traditional yoga while keeping presence alive (allowing yourself to enjoy can still be quite challenging!) A little dance is welcome too. I'll describe it in more detail at the event.

## Cost

This event is unconditionally given. That means it’s a gift to the world from me, and I have no expectation of reciprocity (If I do somehow, please contact me with your feedback.)

I make this happen by personally funding my events as well as spending a huge amount of (unseen) time on organizing.

I welcome energy exchange. I’ll lovingly accept your gift of food or money. Perhaps you'd like to offer me a massage or sound healing. Teach or lend me a skill, offer me to use your canoe during summer or whatever else you come up with 😇.

The best way to offer energy exchange is is to send me an e-mail (and let me know if your offer extends to the spaceholders too).

If you wish to offer money the best way is to give me some cash at the event. I also have a [donation page.](https://buymeacoffee.com/nielsvalent)

And again. Please don't feel pressured to donate. I wish to invert the dynamic. If we receive a lot of love unconditionally, doesn't it become much easier to give as well?
