## What, where and when?

Camping chairs, curiosity and cozyness. It doesn't take much more than that to create a loving bubble for connection. For anyone. 

We'll be setting up a public space where we provide strangers with free listening sessions. Hosts are going around inviting people to listening sessions. If they are interested, they are presented with a 'menu' for the type of listening they wish to receive. The menu depends on the listeners currently available, and could include listening types like: 'silent listening', 'silent listening with impact' or 'active listening'. Duration is also part of the menu - some listeners might feel safer with a time container whereas others might prefer the wide open space :)

It is possible to join this event as a team member (contact me) or you can come by for a free listening session or to just hang out. 

The event will be at Museumplein (I will update this page with the exact location at the start of the event.) from 11.00 - ~13.00.
