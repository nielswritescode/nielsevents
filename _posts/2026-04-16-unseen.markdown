What does it take to organize unconditional events? 

- Sustainable promotion (Digital, interpersonal, on the street)
- Networking 
- Keeping up my own practice
- Following facilitation trainings
- Keeping up IT infrastructure
- Dealing with no-shows / latecomers etc.
- Integrate critical feedback
- Bringing materials, snacks etc to events
- A lot of relational work
- Being available to others
- and much more...

Even a small event takes at least a couple of hours to organize. And that is disregarding the initial 1000?+ hours investment.
