## What, where and when?

From 13.00 - 15.00 on 28th of September. Location will likely be Science Park, but we're still exploring options. Check this page for updates or sign up to be automatically notified.

A facilitated space for exploring deeper, real connection
through platonic authentic relating games.

If you don't know what AR is, there are many great [resources](https://authenticrelating.co/what-is-ar/) online to get an idea about it.
Our interpretation of it currently tends to be a bit more on the playful side, although there
can be a lot of depth to that.

Feel free to contact us by e-mail if you have any questions. 

> nielsevents@proton.me

## Rules

The space is experimental. We are just starting out with hosting this type 
of event (though we have a lot of combined experience in many related fields).
Therefore we have set up a couple guidelines:

- We are responsible for the space together. We take a lot of care and effort
to facilitate it safely, but we expect you to stay aware of your own boundaries as well as those of others.
- We can't hold the space for sexuality or deep trauma. Of course this
is welcome to show up in your authentic experience, but in that case please find
a way to take a breath, soften it up or even step out of the game for a bit. 

## Unconditionally given

The event is unconditionally given. That means this event is a gift to the world
from us, and we have no expectation of reciprocity.

If you do feel called to support us (and again, only if you feel so, there are no judgements) you could:

- Become a member of [studentmeditation](https://www.studentmeditation.nl/become-a-member/) (15.50 for a year)
- Leave a cash donation with us
- Support the organization or us personally with your skills (surprise us <3)
