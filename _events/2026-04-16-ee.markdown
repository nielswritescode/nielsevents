Currently the concept of energy exchange (EE) seems to be quite limited. EE Options to participants attending events are usually limited to:

- Helping out at the event
- Offering money or bringing snacks etc.
- Offering their own practice (already rare)

I believe the concept of energy exchange could be much more flexible than this. 

Perhaps you have some canoes that I could borrow in summer. You organize events and I can come for free. You offer massages. You have a nice IT tool that I can use. You can help me sell personal items. You can offer some transport with a van if I ever have to move. You're good at fixing bikes. You have access to spaces. You can offer me psychotherapy sessions. 

The options are endless....

And they can be:

- Ultraspecific (massage for 5 minutes on 28th of May in Amsterdam Zuid if I plan it between now and a week)
- At any point in time (now or in a year, does it really matter?)
- To other people (my mother needs massages as well!)
- Non-specific (You provide in general an hour of your time in service to me for a range of activities)

Just let me know what you'd like to offer :) I'm open to the unexpected.
