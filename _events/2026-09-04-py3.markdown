_A held space for authentic unguided movement & relaxation, in connection._

## The vibe 🌊

I see pleasure yoga as deeply listening to the body. Only moving it when it tells me to move, and allowing myself to enjoy this process. It often sends me into an illogical mix of stillness, self-massage, nervous system stimulation and dance. Sometimes I want to sleep, sometimes to laugh. Make some noises and stare at a wall. 

Allowing myself to enjoy is not an easy practice. There is this energy of needing discipline to practice - I personally think practicing moving out of self-love is already requiring quite a lot of discipline. And it actually yields so many beautiful things and motivations to move & practice more...

## The structure 🧑‍🏫

I facilitate an opening and a closing. In the opening I set a (very wide) context for the practice. I also let us connect so as to do the practice together (even though it's mostly individual). I leave some space for intention setting. At the end we wrap up together with some small sharing.
