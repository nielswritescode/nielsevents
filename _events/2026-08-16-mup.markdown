_Meditating in various places that exert some type of discomfort._

## The vibe 🌊
A lot of us spend a lot of time in noisy, busy and unsightly environments. Is there something we could learn from consciously bringing the practice to these places? It is possible to learn to change our relationship to discomfort?

I recall a day when I was super lethargic from the heat, on my way to some event. I then decided to meditate in the hottest, noisiest spot possible. A bridge over a highway. It felt challenging at first - the lethargy, heat and noise. The people asking me if I am okay by just sitting in the street there. But after accepting these things I grew into really being okay with being there, even feeling ecstatic. All the next environments that I biked through after that were peanuts compared to that and I found myself being in great joy despite the heat. Just an example of what this practice could do.

## The structure 🧑‍🏫
We will gather at a safe spot. I will welcome you into the space and offer a moment for intention setting. We'll walk towards the meditation spot and sit there for 30 minutes. I'll signal the end and we'll walk back to the safe spot where we will do some group debrief.
